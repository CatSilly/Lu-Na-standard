# LNS Scoring System
This is an overall score to indicate whether the product is good or bad.

| Score | Rating |
|------:|--------|
| < 50 | Terrible |
| 50 – 69 | Fair |
| 70 – 79 | Good |
| 80 – 89 | Great |
| 90 – 99 | Outstanding |
| 100 | Absolutely impossible |

> [!NOTE]
> A score below 50 doesn't necessarily mean the project/product is terrible, it's just a personal assessment.

> [!NOTE]
> This may change in the future for balancing.
